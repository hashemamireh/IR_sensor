Five icon concepts for the tablet app

Each concept sits on a dark and a light launcher background. Sizes are in points, the way the macOS Dock and GNOME count them. The pixel views on the right are true 32×32 and 16×16 rasters, blown up, which is what the 32x32.png and icon.ico files will contain.

Shape

The macOS shape follows Apple's grid: an 824-point plate centred on a 1024 canvas, corners at 22.5%, with a soft shadow inside the canvas. Tauri ships the same PNG to Linux, where GNOME shows it as is, so the rounded square also works on Fedora. Click any large icon to see the 1024 master.
